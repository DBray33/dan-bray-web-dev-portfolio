About Me

From Craft Beer to Code

I'm Dan Bray, a motivated and detail-oriented professional with a passion for creativity and technology. After 8 years of excelling in the craft beer industry, I've transitioned my skills and experience into a career in web development and digital design.

Throughout my career, I've honed my abilities in project management, web and graphic design, social media strategy, and e-commerce development. At Broken Chair Brewery, I managed every facet of operations, from crafting award-winning beer recipes to spearheading digital marketing campaigns.

I'm currently advancing my web development skills through certifications and hands-on projects. My portfolio showcases my commitment to crafting impactful digital solutions while enhancing my proficiency in the MERN stack, WordPress, and custom-coded websites.
